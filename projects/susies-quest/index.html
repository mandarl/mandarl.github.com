How to Play

🎮 Controls

⬅️ ➡️ Tap the arrow buttons to move left or right
⬆️ Susie jumps automatically on platforms
💡 TIP: Move while jumping to jump higher!

🎯 Objective

🧶 Collect yarn balls for +100 points
🍭 Collect candy for +150 points
⚠️ Avoid spiky enemies or lose a life!

🌟 Power-Ups

🛡️ Shield - Protects from one enemy hit
🧲 Magnet - Attracts nearby collectibles
2️⃣ Double Points - 2x score for a limited time

🪵 Platform Types

🟫 Normal - Standard platform
🟩 Moving - Slides left and right
☁️ Cloud - Disappears after one jump
💗 Bouncy - Extra high jump!
🟧 Breakable - Crumbles after landing
